## Development

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.

## UI/UX Pro Max & 21st.dev Design Standards (Cotto Napoliten Edition)

This project follows the **UI/UX Pro Max $25,000 Agency Standard** (inspired by 21st.dev, Framer Motion, and Artisanal Neapolitan Gastronomy):

- **Brand Palette:** Warm Neapolitan flour/stone backgrounds (`#FBF8F3`, `#F4EDE4`), volcanic Vesuvius charcoal (`#141210`, `#0D0B0A`), San Marzano tomato & terracotta fire (`#D9381E`, `#E04F33`), golden crust amber (`#E59838`, `#F59E0B`), and fresh basil green (`#16A34A`).
- **Typography:** Editorial display headings (`Syne` / `Fraunces` + `Plus Jakarta Sans`) paired with `JetBrains Mono` for financial ROI metrics, oven temperatures (`450°C`), fermentation hours (`72 Saat`), and tracking codes.
- **Franchise First Architecture:** Interactive ROI & Investment Calculator, 3 Franchise Concept Tiers (Express, Bistro, Flagship Gastro), Zero-Master-Chef Fornero Hybrid Stone Oven Advantage, Expansion Map, and Full HQ Franchise CRM Portal (`/admin`).
